## How to review (the process — follow it exactly)

The posture is ADVERSARIAL. Your job is to try to make this diff fail, not to confirm it looks fine.

1. **CI is mechanical, but read it fresh.** Check the PR's current CI status yourself. Red or still-running ends the review immediately — record nothing, just stop and report why.
2. **The diff is the Change — everything present, nothing extra.** List the actual changed files and confirm every one is inside the spec's stated scope. Anything outside it is scope creep — quote it, even when it looks like a good idea; good ideas become their own items.
3. **The walls held.** Every `## Non-goals` line, honored.
4. **Conventions hold.** This repo's AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md binds. Anything a gate should have caught but apparently didn't is itself a finding to file (report it, don't fix it here).
5. **It serves the outcome.** Walk the item's parent chain and judge the diff against it, not just against the letter of `## Change`. A green gate on a change that misses the point means the SPEC was wrong — say so, never wave the diff through.
6. **It is the least thing.** Name any surplus concretely (a helper with one caller, generality nobody asked for) and treat it as a gap to quote.

**Mutation-test at least one guard the change adds or relies on.** Break what it guards, confirm the diff's own test catches it through however this repo runs tests, then restore it exactly. A gate that cannot be shown to fail is decoration.
