## Evidence — the pattern is proven (2026-08-16)

The first wave found TWO real defects and one instrument defect, all through the
found-bug protocol:

- **url authority promotion** (silent, security-relevant): `format` of an
  authority-less Url whose decoded path starts `//` re-reads as
  protocol-relative — `/%2Fevil.example.com/x` round-trips to
  `host=evil.example.com`. Found by #1129&#39;s fixpoint property at iteration
  18,602. Fix slice: #1161 (ready); #1129 blocked on it.
- **json float loss**: the C encoder serializes floats in ~[1e17, 2^63) to
  integer-shaped tokens the decoder integer-parses — `decode(encode(x)) ~= x`
  silently. Found by #1128&#39;s round-trip property. Fix filed on the C core&#39;s
  board: whilp/cosmopolitan#265; #1128&#39;s property bounds floats below 2^53
  until the fix arrives via a pin bump.
- **the deep-run acceptance was vacuous**: `--make test` replays a cached
  verdict when only `FUZZ_ITERS` changed (#1156, shaping — env-stamp design
  leaning recorded there). Interim: every fuzz slice&#39;s acceptance deletes the
  `.got` markers explicitly and quotes both runs&#39; timings.

## The found-bug protocol (binding on every child)

A fuzz property that fails against CURRENT behavior is a finding, not a test to
weaken: the implementer comments the failing seed + base64 input on their issue,
moves it to shaping, and stops. The planner decides fix vs deliberate contract, and a
confirmed bug becomes its own issue plus a regression test. Never adjust a property
or a generator to make a real failure stop reproducing. (Both generator bounds in
#1128 are PLANNER-settled contract decisions with rationale recorded — the
protocol&#39;s legitimate outcome, not a workaround.)
