
## The journal

Keep `JOURNAL.md` in this directory, as you go, not from memory at the
end. It is the most important deliverable; write it even if the rest
does not work. You may be stopped at any moment, without warning, and
what is in the file then is all that is kept. It has two parts:

**Summary**, at the top of the file from your first entry, and rewritten
after every log entry so that it always matches the log below it:

- what works and what does not, against the list above
- the things that most slowed you down, ranked, with the entry numbers
  they refer to
- what you guessed rather than learned from the tool, and whether the
  guess held up
- what you never figured out
- the one change to the tool that would have helped you most

**Log**, below the summary: one entry per thing you tried, in order. Each
entry says what you were trying to find out or do, the exact command you
ran, what came back (quote the real output, trimmed), what you expected
instead if it differed, and what you concluded. Include dead ends.

Once the deliverables are done and the summary is current, stop; do not
re-verify what you have already checked. Stop too once you have been
stuck for a long time with nothing new to try; in that case say so in
the journal and stop.
