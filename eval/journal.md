
## The journal

Keep `JOURNAL.md` in this directory, as you go, not from memory at the
end. It is the most important deliverable; write it even if the rest
does not work. It has two parts:

**Log**: one entry per thing you tried, in order. Each entry says what
you were trying to find out or do, the exact command you ran, what came
back (quote the real output, trimmed), what you expected instead if it
differed, and what you concluded. Include dead ends.

**Summary**, at the end:

- what works and what does not, against the list above
- the things that most slowed you down, ranked, with the entry numbers
  they refer to
- what you guessed rather than learned from the tool, and whether the
  guess held up
- what you never figured out
- the one change to the tool that would have helped you most

Stop once the deliverables are done, or once you have been stuck for a
long time with nothing new to try; in that case say so in the journal
and stop.
