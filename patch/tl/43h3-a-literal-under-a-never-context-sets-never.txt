note: the literal under a context that never returns sets `@never`, so a `return` in it is refused (43g2)
--- find
      self:add_var(nil, "@never", a_type(node, node.never and "any" or "nil", {}))
--- replace
      self:add_var(nil, "@never", a_type(node, (node.never or node.context_never) and "any" or "nil", {}))
--- end
