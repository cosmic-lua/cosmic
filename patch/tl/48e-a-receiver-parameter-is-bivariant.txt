note: the first parameter of a function-typed record field, the receiver, may be narrower than the field's type declares, as it may be wider: an implementation is declared `read: function(FromStringReader, ...)` for an interface's `read: function(Reader, ...)` and is only ever called with the record that holds it (`r:read()`, `r.read(r)`), so the narrower receiver is the one it is given. Every other parameter, and the first of a function that is not a field's, is contravariant (48a)
