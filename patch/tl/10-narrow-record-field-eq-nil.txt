note: `x.field == nil` / `~= nil` is the same exact type test 03-eq-nil.txt gives a bare variable, generalized to a field key -- but only when the field's own declared type admits nil. A field declared with a plain, non-union type, compared to nil anyway as an uninitialized-sentinel pattern, must not narrow to bare `nil`: the double negation the early-return shape feeds this into resolves straight to the positive IsFact (bypassing not_facts's subtract, which is what protects a bare variable's own nil-union case), and a plain type has no nil member to subtract
