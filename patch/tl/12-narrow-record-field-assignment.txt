note: a write to `x` or `x.field` drops the field's narrow before the LHS is even visited, not after the whole statement is checked -- type_check_index (patched by 09-narrow-record-field-dot.txt) substitutes any live narrow the moment the LHS is visited to derive the assignment's own expected type (children[1], read below in `after`), so dropping from `after` would be too late: the mismatch against the stale narrowed type is already raised by the time `after` runs. A `before` callback fires ahead of every child visit, which is the only hook early enough. `x = ...` drops every `x.*` field narrow; `x.field = ...` drops just that one
