"x"` for a `string | nil` was accepted and raised at run time when `s` was nil. The refusal is the operator's own message, which the hint in build/teal.tl turns into the check that drops the nil. `==` and `~=` compare nil fine, and `and`, `or` and `not` take any value, so they are left alone; `any`, an unknown and a type variable are not unions with nil, and keep passing
