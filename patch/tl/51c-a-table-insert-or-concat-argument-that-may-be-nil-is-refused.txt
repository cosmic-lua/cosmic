note: an argument of `table.insert` or `table.concat` whose type is a union carrying nil is refused where the parameter, with its type variables bound by the arguments before it, does not take nil. nil is a subtype of every type, so `table.insert(list, name)` for a `string | nil` was accepted: it appends nothing and `table.concat` raises at run time on a nil element or list. These two are named because they are the standard calls that fail in this way and every site they hit was measured and fixed; any other call keeps accepting nil for a parameter that does not take it (doc/roadmap.md has the rest). A parameter that is a type variable not yet bound takes the argument's type, nil included, and one of `any` or a union with nil takes it too
