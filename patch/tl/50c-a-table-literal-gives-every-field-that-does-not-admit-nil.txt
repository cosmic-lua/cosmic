note: Records are open, so `local r: R = {}` type-checked for an `R` with `name: string` and `r.name` was nil. A table literal checked against a record or an interface must give every field whose type does not admit nil (01-nil-union.txt's admits_nil: `name?: T` and `name: T | nil` may be omitted), and is refused otherwise, naming the type and the fields. Not required, because a literal does not supply them: a field of function type (a callback, a generic function, an overload; a method is supplied by `__index`), a method declared in the record body, a nested type declaration, and a metamethod key. 50b and `Env.lenient_literals` (50a) exempt a literal. `<total>` is unchanged: it asks for every field, the optional ones too
