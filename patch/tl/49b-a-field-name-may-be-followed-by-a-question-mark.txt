note: `name?: T` in a record or interface body: the `?` after a field's name, written before its `:`, marks it optional (49c gives it its type). It follows a name or a `["string"]` key alike. Only an ordinary field can be optional: a `metamethod` is looked up by the runtime, which has no use for a field that may be absent in a declaration that says what it holds. A `?` that no `:` follows is left for the error that already names the `:` expected
