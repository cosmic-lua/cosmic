file: std/Build/Cache.zig
note: std.Build.Cache's checkInputPath re-hashes a direct input whose size, mtime or inode moved, then compares the new hash to the caller's digest of it, which addInputPath never computes unless given one: every such input reads as changed contents, so a fresh checkout or an extracted cache archive, whose files all have new inodes, misses every step reading one. This record keeps the recorded digest before a re-hash overwrites it; the next three compare to it. Upstream: branch cache-same-contents-hit of cosmic-lua/zig, with its tests.
