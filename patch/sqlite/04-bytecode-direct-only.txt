note: bytecode() and tables_used() compile the SQL they are given, and compiling a PRAGMA that sets something sets it: `bytecode('PRAGMA query_only = 0')` turns query_only off, and `PRAGMA temp_store_directory` changes the whole process. Neither table is marked direct-only upstream, so a view or trigger in a database a caller opens could change the connection's settings just by being read. Marked SQLITE_VTAB_DIRECTONLY, as cosmic's own SQL functions are SQLITE_DIRECTONLY, the tables answer the caller's own SQL and refuse a view's or a trigger's ("unsafe use of virtual table"). Upstream marks neither as of 3.54.0.
