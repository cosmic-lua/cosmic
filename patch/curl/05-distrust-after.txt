file: lib/vtls/mbedtls.c
note: Mozilla stops trusting a root for server certificates issued
  after a date (CKA_NSS_SERVER_DISTRUST_AFTER in NSS's certdata.txt),
  which no list of roots can say. cosmic's core keeps those dates with
  the roots it carries (core/http.c, build/roots.tl), and the verify
  callback asks it of each certificate of a chain: the server's own is
  refused (MBEDTLS_X509_BADCERT_OTHER) when its root is one of them and
  it was issued after the date. It refuses too any certificate of the
  chain Mozilla's OneCRL revokes (MBEDTLS_X509_BADCERT_REVOKED; the core
  carries OneCRL's entries, build/onecrl.tl). The core defines
  cosmic_tls_distrust; curl here only declares and calls it.
