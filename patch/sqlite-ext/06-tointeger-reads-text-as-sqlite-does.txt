file: ext/misc/totype.c
note: tointeger() of a text takes an integer literal alone, a sign and digits, as upstream does, but reads it with SQLite's own reading (05-totype-reads-text-as-sqlite-does.txt) in place of the copied parser, which stops at a NUL and reads '12\0x' as 12. A literal past the 64-bit range reads as a real, and is NULL. Text with a point or an exponent is NULL even when it names a whole number: SQLite reads it through a real, which keeps about 19 digits, so '1.00000000000000001' would read as 1.
